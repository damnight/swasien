### Celestial Mark

- You can activate your celestial mark as a bonus action, transforming you into your celestial form. This effect lasts for one minute and you can activate it only once per longrest. The effect lasts for the duration and ends only when you die or when you end it as a bonus action.
    

Abilities:

- While active you gain the following benefits:
    

- Your maximum hit points are increased by 50.When you die you can use your celestial mark to consume the soul which is embedded in the celestial mark. When you do so all conditions and points of exhaustion ends on you, you regain 100 temporary hit points and you can take your turn in the round immediately. Your mark becomes dormant when no soul is embedded in the mark. When you consume the celestial soul the mark is destroyed.
    
- You can use an action to detect celestial creatures and celestial marks within 500 km of you. You immediately know which domain the creature originates from.
    

- Each time you activate the celestial mark you can choose one of the following options:
    

- Olituras Trickery: While in your celestial form you can roll a d4 when you take damage once per round. When you roll a 3 or 4 you take no damage instead. In addition you are immune to the charmed condition and any effect that would sense your emotions, or read your thoughts.
    
- Devas Life: While in your celestial form you regain hit points equal to your proficiency bonus at the start of your turn as long as you have one hit point. In addition you have resistance against radiant damage and advantage on saving throws caused by celestial creatures.
    
- Irontons Forge: While in your celestial form you gain resistance against one of the following damage types: bludgeoning, piercing or slashing and you deal double damage to constructs and objects. In addition armor and weapons you wear or hold cannot be destroyed.
    
- Morathirs Nature: While in your celestial form beast and plant creatures regard you as an ally, follow your commands on their turn and can't attack you. In addition you can restrain a creature once per round when you damage them. They stay restrained until the end of their next turn.
    
- Kiaransalees Death: While in your celestial form your attacks deal additional damage equal to your proficiency bonus. In addition you have resistance against necrotic damage and advantage on saving throws caused by undead creatures.
    
- Faktus Order: While you are in your celestial form you can treat one of your d20 rolls as a 10 after you see the initial roll once per turn. In addition creatures can't get advantage on attack rolls against you or their saving throws against your abilities or spells.
    

- Mark Evolution:
    

- To evolve your celestial mark you must embed an additional 500 points of divine soul energy in your mark. When evolving, the celestial mark becomes permanent and your creature type changes to celestial. In addition you gain three unique benefits depending on which domain originates from the soul energy. When you interrupt the evolution the soul energy needs to be contained into an object or a vessel or vanishes to the afterlife.
    

### Demi-God

If your Celestials Mark contains 1100 celestial souls you can try to undergo a transformation into a Demi God. As a Demi-God you gain the following traits and benefits:

- You transform into a celestial Demi God of a Divine Domain and stop being a humanoid creature. As you transform, your true form and your appearance changes to better suit your domain.
    
- You are no longer native to the Prime Material Plane as you become a part of your domain. As a Demi God you don't suffer mortal limitations and can only be destroyed, if you are killed in your own domain.
    
- Your new limitation for ability scores is 30 and all your ability scores are increased by +5.
